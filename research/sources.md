# Manlius local research
Checked October 5, 2026.

Manlius town and Manlius village are different jurisdictions. The <a href="https://www.villageofminoa.gov/1192/Waste-Water-Treatment">Village of Minoa documents its own wastewater system</a>. Confirm the property's connection and responsible utility before treating a blockage as a private drain issue.

The village historian records incorporation in 1813 and a historic district entered on the National Register in 1973. After World War Two, water service expanded and a developer-built sewage treatment system later became publicly operated.
https://www.manliusvillageny.gov/history

Minoa operates its own wastewater treatment facility. The village describes a collection system with 28.5 miles of sewer and one main pump station. A village system is not proof of the condition of an individual private lateral.
https://www.villageofminoa.gov/1192/Waste-Water-Treatment
